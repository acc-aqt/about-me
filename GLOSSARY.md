# Glossary

- **Player**: the pixel Alexander-with-camera the visitor controls.
- **Overworld**: the single top-down map the Player explores tile by tile. Avoid: "Route", "level".
- **Hub**: Munich, the central Zone where the Player spawns and from which a path leads to every other Zone; the Contact mailbox stands in the Hub itself.
- **Zone**: a themed region of the Overworld reached from the Hub (Career road, Maker workshop, Photography trail, Sport, Music & games, Contact).
- **Point of Interest (POI)**: an in-world object the Player can photograph to reveal content. Avoid: "hotspot", "trigger".
- **Signpost**: a sign beside a path out of the Hub, naming the Zones that path leads to. Not a POI: the Shutter does nothing at it.
- **Name plate**: the small label above a POI or Signpost naming it, shown only while the Player is near. Avoid: "tooltip".
- **Shutter**: the action (Space / on-screen button) that photographs a POI.
- **Viewfinder**: the overlay opened by the Shutter that shows a POI's content and full-resolution photos. Avoid: "modal", "popup".
- **Fast-travel**: menu to jump the Player to any Zone.
- **Plain view**: the non-game scrolling version of the site. Avoid: "fallback", "lite mode".
- **Content file**: the single typed source of all Zone/POI content, read by both the game and the Plain view.
- **Inventory**: the skills screen (key `I`).
- **Film roll**: a hidden collectible, one per Zone.
- **Handheld**: the Game Boy-inspired device shell that frames the game in portrait, holding the screen, d-pad and A/B/START/SELECT buttons. Avoid: "Game Boy", "frame", "skin".
