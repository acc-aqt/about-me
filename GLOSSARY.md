# Glossary

- **Player**: the pixel Alexander-with-camera the visitor controls.
- **Route**: the single linear left-to-right walk through all Zones.
- **Zone**: a themed stretch of the Route (Career road, Maker workshop, Photography trail, Sport, Music & games, Contact).
- **Point of Interest (POI)**: an in-world object the Player can photograph to reveal content. Avoid: "hotspot", "trigger".
- **Shutter**: the action (Space / on-screen button) that photographs a POI.
- **Viewfinder**: the overlay opened by the Shutter that shows a POI's content and full-resolution photos. Avoid: "modal", "popup".
- **Fast-travel**: menu to jump the Player to any Zone.
- **Plain view**: the non-game scrolling version of the site. Avoid: "fallback", "lite mode".
- **Content file**: the single typed source of all Zone/POI content, read by both the game and the Plain view.
- **Inventory**: the skills screen (key `I`).
- **Film roll**: a hidden collectible, one per Zone.
