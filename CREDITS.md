# Credits

## Pixel Platformer tiles — Kenney

`public/art/kenney-pixel-platformer-tiles.png` is the packed tilemap from
[Pixel Platformer](https://kenney.nl/assets/pixel-platformer) (v1.2) by Kenney (www.kenney.nl),
downloaded from the [OpenGameArt mirror](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/kenney_pixel-platformer.zip).
It provides the ground, signs and small props.

Attribution is not required; it is given here with thanks.

## Original art

The Player (photographer) sprite, the Film roll, the skies and all backdrops — the city skyline at the start,
the hangar with the jet engine, the mountains and the other Zone scenery — are original pixel art painted in code
in `src/art.ts`. The pixel font used for in-world labels is an original 5×9 font drawn in `src/pixel-font.ts`.
