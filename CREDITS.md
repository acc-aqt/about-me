# Credits

## Tiny Town tiles — Kenney

`public/art/kenney-tiny-town-tiles.png` is the packed tilemap from
[Tiny Town](https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town) (v1.1) by Kenney (www.kenney.nl),
downloaded from the [OpenGameArt mirror](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/kenney_tiny-town.zip).
It provides the Overworld's grass, paths, trees, bushes, fences, buildings and signs.

## Original art

The Player (photographer) sprite, the Film roll, the water, the Hub maypole and the themed Points of Interest
(such as the Contact mailbox and the Career road buildings) are original pixel art painted in code
in `src/art.ts`. The pixel font used for in-world labels is an original 5×9 font drawn in `src/pixel-font.ts`.
